# Human-Discovered Regression Cases

These cases came from Discovery v2.x human validation and must remain permanent regressions.

## HR-01 — Premature broad-driver closure
Member raises Money plus Energy/physical concerns. Engine must not declare Discovery resolved merely because broad state scores become high. Each member-raised signal must reach a terminal state and required driver/scope evidence must be present.

## HR-02 — Finance multi-source loss
Member has financial pressure from multiple simultaneous sources such as no income/unemployment + debt + expenses. All applicable causes must be preserved. A generic `several things` answer is not sufficient representation.

## HR-03 — Repeated uncertainty
Member raises Focus/Motivation but answers `Not sure` to causal/interpretive probes. Engine must pivot to observable impact/frequency/recent-behavior questions. It must not wander across adjacent causal hypotheses until the global ceiling. If cause remains unknowable, state may be confirmed while driver terminates `unresolved`.

Forbidden outcome: a meaningful driver conclusion based primarily on association/propagation.

## HR-04 — Healthy member gateway
Member reports that none of the listed problems are bothering them.

Required behavior:
- `None of these / Generally well` is available and distinct from `Not sure`.
- Problem investigation does not manufacture a deficit.
- Route toward what the member wants EL8 to help with: maintenance, goal, habit/routine, selected wellness area, or exploration.

## HR-05 — Work without Money
Member selects Work/School. First follow-up may clarify Work/School. Member provides no evidence of financial pressure.

Forbidden behavior: Money branch becomes authorized merely because Work and Money are associated.

## HR-06 — Relationships + Home + Focus without Health
Member selects Relationships, Home/Surroundings, and Focus/Motivation. Member provides no health/energy evidence.

Forbidden behavior: asking `When you say health or energy, what feels relevant?` or otherwise opening Health/Energy solely from graph association.

## HR-07 — Contradiction after Work selection
Member selects Work/School, then indicates `nothing major` in the first scope question.

Required behavior: clarify the apparent contradiction or allow the member to clear/narrow the Work signal.

Forbidden behavior: silently topic-switching to Money or assuming Work means employment/income rather than school or another work-related issue.

## HR-08 — Short clear Sleep route
Member selects Sleep, clearly confirms a meaningful sleep problem, and provides sufficient relevant evidence.

Required behavior: allow a short route when the signal is adequately understood. Do not ask extra questions merely to reach a quota.

## HR-09 — Unknown driver is valid
Member clearly reports a state/problem but cannot identify its cause after bounded observable follow-up.

Required output example:
- state: confirmed
- driver: unresolved

Forbidden behavior: treating inability to identify a cause as proof that the state itself is uncertain, or selecting the highest associated driver as fact.

## HR-10 — Member-raised signal permanence
Member raises several independent concerns. One becomes highly confident early.

Forbidden behavior: closing Discovery while another material member-raised signal remains non-terminal.
